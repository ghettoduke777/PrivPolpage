01

Information we collect & local storage

We believe in data minimization. Your core personal data and app content are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to or stored on external servers or cloud databases.

  • Calorie and event logs

    Any text, numbers, dates, or meal descriptions you manually enter are saved strictly on your device’s internal storage.

  • Camera and photo library data

    With your permission, the app uses your camera to capture food or event photos, or accesses your gallery so you can select images for your logs. These photos remain on your device and are not uploaded to an external server.

  • Image metadata

    Any location or time metadata attached to your photos is processed entirely on-device to help organize your logs.

04

Permissions & control

You have full control over your device permissions and can change them at any time through your Android settings (Settings > Apps > [Insert App Name] > Permissions):

  • Camera & storage: Revoking these permissions will prevent you from adding photo logs, but you will still be able to manually type in your text and calorie data.
  • Personalized ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising by resetting or turning off your Advertising ID in your Android system settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
05

Children’s privacy

Our app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect personal logs on external servers, we do not knowingly gather or store data from children. Ad networks utilized by the app are configured to comply with child safety laws (such as COPPA).

06

Changes to this policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or ad network requirements. We will notify you of any changes by updating the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy and making the text accessible within the app.
